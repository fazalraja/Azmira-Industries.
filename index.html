The Soul of Amroha Crafts

Where Master Hands Breathe Life Into Natural Wood & Metal

For centuries, the soil of District Amroha has echoed with the rhythmic tapping of chisels, the hum of wood lathes, and the warm glow of brass casting furnaces.

At Azmira Industries, located at Sambhal Road, Hasanpur, we carry this timeless legacy forward. We blend indigenous Indian craftsmanship with contemporary sophistication to produce museum-grade artifacts, statement furniture, sacred brassware, and bespoke architectural fixtures.

Seasoned Timber

Sheesham, Teak, Acacia, and Mango woods thoroughly kiln-dried for lasting durability without warping.

Generational Ustads

Hand-carved by master artisans possessing ancestral expertise in Mughal jaali, inlay, and metal repoussé.

Export & OEM Supply

Serving interior designers, boutique luxury hotels, wholesale exporters, and discerning homeowners worldwide.

Pure Organic Finishes

Natural beeswax, food-safe oils, and antiqued patinas that celebrate raw organic wood grains.

Ancestral Techniques

The Journey of Every Artifact

Each creation is a labor of love that takes days of devoted attention from master craftsmen in Hasanpur.

01

Timber Selection

We handpick ethically harvested, mature Sheesham, Acacia, and Mango woods, ensuring natural seasoning and rich grain figures.

02

Freehand Blueprinting

Intricate floral arabesques, jaali lattices, and geometric motifs are drawn directly onto the wood surface without automated stencils.

03

Artisanal Chiseling

Using centuries-old steel gouges and mallets, our master carvers sculpt multi-dimensional relief work with surgical precision.

04

Hand Buff & Patina

Hand-rubbed with natural organic waxes, oils, or antiqued brass washes that protect the artifact while deepening with age.
